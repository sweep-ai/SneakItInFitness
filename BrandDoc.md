Core pain points (buyer psychology)
Stuck in a loop. They've started and stopped fitness routines multiple times. They don't lack motivation — they lack structure and accountability. The problem they buy around is: "I know what to do but I don't do it."

Eating "clean" but not getting lean. One of Ari's strongest hooks. This ICP believes they're doing the right things but aren't seeing results. The real issue is usually calories/protein, not food quality — and Ari calls it out directly.

Life is busy and fitness always loses. Job, social life, and stress push gym to last priority. They feel guilty but don't know how to build a sustainable system around their actual life.

Confidence/identity is the real issue. Underneath the body goals is a person who doesn't feel proud of who they are. They want to look in the mirror and respect themselves. Ari's own story mirrors this exactly — makes him uniquely credible here.

Cope and avoidance. They've either been told "it's okay to be where you are" (body positivity messaging) or they're telling themselves that. Ari calls this out bluntly — "you can't run from reality" — and it triggers deep resonance with buyers who feel the gap between current and desired self.
